1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
--------------------
Aetheria Dynamics Inc. ("the Company") is committed to maintaining a safe,
healthy, and productive working environment for all employees, contractors,
visitors, and stakeholders. This Health & Safety Policy applies across all
corporate offices, research laboratories, hardware testing facilities, and
remote work environments operated by Aetheria Dynamics Inc.

Compliance with these procedures is a condition of employment. Every team
member shares responsibility for identifying hazards, adhering to safety
protocols, and promoting a proactive safety culture.


2. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
-----------------------------
2.1 Executive Leadership & Safety Committee
- Allocate necessary financial and operational resources to maintain workplace
  safety standards.
- Conduct quarterly safety audits and review incident reports through the
  Corporate Health & Safety Committee (CHSC).
- Ensure full compliance with applicable occupational health and safety
  regulations.

2.2 Department Managers & Team Leads
- Ensure all new hires and contractors complete mandatory safety onboarding
  prior to accessing workspaces or equipment.
- Enforce daily adherence to Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) requirements
  and operational Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs).
- Promptly investigate and document all workplace hazards, near-misses, and
  injuries within their teams.

2.3 Employees & Contractors
- Follow all posted safety instructions, equipment manuals, and emergency
  procedures.
- Immediately report unsafe conditions, damaged equipment, or suspicious
  activity to a supervisor or the Safety Desk.
- Refrain from operating specialized machinery, high-voltage systems, or
  chemical agents without documented certification.


3. WORKPLACE & FACILITY SAFETY PROTOCOLS
----------------------------------------
3.1 General Office & Ergonomic Standards
- Workstation Setup: Employees are entitled to an ergonomic assessment upon
  hire and annually thereafter. Monitors should be positioned at eye level,
  and adjustable seating must be utilized properly.
- Walkways & Exits: All corridors, stairwells, fire exits, and electrical
  panels must remain completely unobstructed at all times. Storage of boxes or
  cables in walkways is strictly prohibited.
- Electrical Safety: Daisy-chaining power strips or surge protectors is
  forbidden. Frayed cords or malfunctioning outlets must be tagged out and
  reported to Facilities immediately.

3.2 Hardware Labs & High-Voltage Testing Zones
- Access Control: Entry to Restricted Engineering Labs (Zones B and C) requires
  Level-2 badge clearance and completion of the High-Voltage & Electrostatic
  Discharge (ESD) Safety Module.
- Personal Protective Equipment (PPE): Safety glasses (ANSI Z87.1 rated),
  ESD-safe wrist straps, and closed-toe shoes are mandatory inside active
  hardware testing bays.
- Lockout/Tagout (LOTO): Before performing maintenance or calibration on
  energized equipment exceeding 50V, technicians must execute full
  Lockout/Tagout procedures and verify zero-energy states.
- Battery & Thermal Testing: Lithium-ion battery stress tests must occur
  exclusively inside ventilated blast-containment enclosures equipped with
  Class D fire suppression systems.

3.3 Remote & Hybrid Work Safety
- Remote employees must maintain a dedicated, hazard-free home workspace with
  adequate lighting, ventilation, and grounded electrical outlets.
- Work-related injuries sustained during designated working hours in a home
  office must be reported to Human Resources within 24 hours, following
  standard incident reporting guidelines.


4. EMERGENCY PROCEDURES & EVACUATION
------------------------------------
4.1 Fire & Structural Evacuation
1. Alarm Activation: Upon hearing the continuous building alarm or receiving an
   emergency SMS alert, immediately cease all work and power down active bench
   equipment if safe to do so.
2. Evacuation: Proceed calmly to the nearest illuminated emergency exit. Do
   not use elevators.
3. Assembly Point: Report directly to your designated Floor Warden at Assembly
   Zone North (Parking Lot B) for headcount verification.
4. Re-entry: Do not re-enter the facility until the all-clear signal is
   formally issued by Emergency Services and the Chief Safety Officer.

4.2 Medical Emergencies & First Aid
- Automated External Defibrillators (AEDs) and trauma first-aid kits are
  mounted beside the main elevator banks on every floor and outside Lab Zone B.
- In the event of a severe injury, chemical exposure, or medical crisis, dial
  911 (or local emergency services) immediately, then notify Security at
  internal extension #4400.

4.3 Severe Weather & Shelter-in-Place
- During tornado warnings, severe storms, or external hazardous material
  releases, move immediately to interior windowless rooms on the ground floor
  (Rooms 104-112) until official clearance is given.


5. INCIDENT & NEAR-MISS REPORTING
---------------------------------
Aetheria Dynamics operates a non-punitive reporting policy for safety hazards
and near-misses. Learning from close calls prevents future injuries.

- Reporting Timeline: All workplace injuries, illnesses, property damage, and
  near-misses must be logged in the Aetheria Safety Portal
  (safety.aetheria-internal.com) within 24 hours of occurrence.
- Critical Incidents: Any incident requiring hospitalization or involving
  hazardous material spills must be reported verbally to the Environmental
  Health & Safety (EHS) Director within 1 hour.
- Investigation: The EHS team will conduct a root-cause analysis within 5
  business days of any logged incident and publish corrective action updates.


6. HEALTH, WELLNESS & SUBSTANCE POLICY
--------------------------------------
6.1 Drug-Free & Alcohol-Free Workplace
- The manufacture, distribution, possession, or use of illegal drugs or
  unauthorized controlled substances on Company premises or while conducting
  Company business is strictly prohibited.
- Alcohol is prohibited during working hours except at officially sanctioned
  corporate events where moderate consumption is permitted; operating
  machinery or driving on Company business under any level of impairment is
  zero-tolerance.

6.2 Communicable Illness & Sick Leave
- Employees experiencing fever, acute respiratory symptoms, or contagious
  illnesses must remain at home and utilize accrued sick leave or remote work
  arrangements until symptom-free for 24 hours without fever-reducing
  medication.

6.3 Mental Health & Employee Assistance Program (EAP)
- Aetheria Dynamics provides confidential 24/7 counseling, stress management
  resources, and crisis support to all employees and their immediate
  household members via the Aetheria Care EAP (Toll-Free: 1-800-555-0199).


7. POLICY ACKNOWLEDGMENT
------------------------
Violation of safety protocols—including bypassing safety interlocks, disabling
sensors, or failing to wear required PPE in restricted zones—may result in
disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment.

All employees must sign the digital acknowledgment form in the HR Compliance
Portal within 14 days of the Effective Date listed above.
